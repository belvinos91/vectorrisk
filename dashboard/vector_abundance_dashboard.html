Console overview

A probabilistic layer for entomological surveillance

This console runs the temperature–rainfall stochastic logistic model from your manuscript directly in the browser. For any climate state it evaluates the persistence condition, the stationary Gamma abundance law, threshold-exceedance risk, and finite-horizon intervention scenarios — then maps them across focus countries.

Focus countries

Kenya · Cameroon · Mozambique

Three malaria- and arbovirus-relevant settings spanning East, Central and Southern Africa. Additional countries and sub-national units plug into the same data structure.

Core index

Λ = r − δ − ½σ²

The volatility-corrected growth rate. Λ>0 is the stochastic persistence condition — a positive mean growth rate alone is not enough.

Primary output

P(X > x_c)

Probability that stationary abundance exceeds an operational index threshold, from the closed-form Gamma survival function.

Girsanov scenarios & policy

Reweight one set of baseline ecological paths toward a high-abundance drift (λσ) or an intervention drift (−u) over a finite horizon, without changing the diffusion coefficient. The stationary curves are long-run references for each drift.

Reading these scenarios for policy

High-abundance (λ)

A stress/outbreak-favouring drift. Use it to bound how bad abundance could get if conditions shift beyond the baseline — a worst-case surveillance trigger.

Intervention (u)

Control that reduces net growth. The effect scales with u/σ: the same effort achieves less in noisier (higher-σ) environments.

ESS

Effective sample size of the weights. High ESS means the reweighting is trustworthy; very low ESS signals the scenario is too far from baseline to estimate reliably.

Time series

Multi-year daily simulation

Positivity-preserving log-Euler integration of the SDE over the repeating seasonal cycle, at daily resolution for 2–8 years. Export the full daily series for downstream analysis.

Governing equations

The model, written out

Every quantity the console computes, in the same form as the manuscript. This screen is a self-contained explanation of what the dashboard is doing and why.

1 · Stochastic abundance
$$dX_t = X_t\big[a(T_t,R_t) - b(T_t,R_t)X_t\big]\,dt + \sigma(T_t,R_t)\,X_t\,dW_t$$
Adult abundance follows a logistic diffusion with multiplicative environmental noise. Everything downstream is built from the drift coefficients \(a,b\) and the volatility \(\sigma\).
2 · Recruitment
$$r(T,R)=\rho\,\beta_E\,p_E(T)p_L(T)p_P(T)\,S_R(R)\,D_T(T)$$
Effective per-capita recruitment combines egg output, thermal survival of each immature stage, rainfall habitat suitability, and thermal development.
$$p_j(T)=p_{j,\max}\exp\!\Big[-\tfrac{(T-T_{j,\mathrm{opt}})^2}{2w_j^2}\Big]$$
Gaussian thermal response for stage \(j\in\{E,L,P\}\) — intermediate optima, not monotone.
$$S_R(R)=\Big[\max\!\big\{0,\tfrac{4R(R_L-R)}{R_L^2}\big\}\Big]^3$$
Rainfall suitability: zero at drought and at flushing rainfall \(R_L\), peaking at \(R_L/2\). The cube sharpens the optimum.
$$D_T(T)=\frac{z\,e^{-z}}{2z+1},\quad z=\max\{\varepsilon,\,c_1T+c_2\}$$
Temperature-development response entering recruitment.
3 · Mortality & capacity
$$L_A(T)=\max\{L_{\min},\,L_{\max}-q_\delta(T-T_{\delta,\mathrm{opt}})^2\},\quad \delta(T)=\tfrac{1}{L_A(T)}$$
Adult lifespan is unimodal in temperature; daily mortality is its reciprocal.
$$K(T,R)=K_{\min}+K_{\mathrm{amp}}\,S_R(R)\,S_K(T)$$
Environmental carrying capacity. In code, K_max is the increment \(K_{\mathrm{amp}}\); peak capacity is \(K_{\min}+K_{\mathrm{amp}}\).
$$a=r-\delta,\qquad b=\frac{r}{K}$$
4 · Volatility & persistence
$$\sigma(T,R)=\min\!\Big\{\sigma_{\max},\,\sigma_0+\sigma_T\big(\tfrac{T-T_{K,\mathrm{opt}}}{\omega_T}\big)^2+\sigma_R\big(\tfrac{R-R_{\mathrm{opt}}}{R_L}\big)^2\Big\}$$
$$\Lambda=a-\tfrac12\sigma^2>0\quad\text{(stochastic persistence)}$$
The volatility correction \(\sigma^2/2\) can push a nominally suitable environment below the persistence threshold.
5 · Stationary Gamma law
$$p_\infty(x)=\frac{\beta^{\alpha}}{\Gamma(\alpha)}x^{\alpha-1}e^{-\beta x},\quad \alpha=\frac{2a}{\sigma^2}-1,\ \beta=\frac{2b}{\sigma^2}$$
$$\mathbb E[X_\infty]=\frac{\Lambda}{b},\qquad \mathrm{Var}(X_\infty)=\frac{\sigma^2\Lambda}{2b^2}$$
$$\mathbb P(X_\infty>x_c)=\frac{\Gamma(\alpha,\beta x_c)}{\Gamma(\alpha)}$$
Closed-form survival function — the exceedance risk mapped in screen 02, computed here with a regularized upper incomplete Gamma function.
6 · Finite-horizon change of measure
$$Z_\tau=\frac{dQ}{dP}=\exp\!\Big[-\!\int_0^\tau\theta_t\,dW_t^P-\tfrac12\!\int_0^\tau\theta_t^2\,dt\Big]$$
$$a_{Q,\text{high}}=a_P+\lambda\sigma\ (\theta=-\lambda),\qquad a_{Q,\text{int}}=a_P-u\ (\theta=\tfrac{u}{\sigma})$$
Reweights baseline paths toward a high-abundance or intervention scenario. Screen 05 checks \(\mathbb E_Q[\Phi]=\mathbb E_P[Z_\tau\Phi]\) numerically.
Backend for the live/full system
The manuscript pipeline (WorldClim v2.1 ingestion, Natural Earth masking, full-raster \(N{=}10{,}000\) Monte Carlo, GeoTIFF export) is Python. To make the console "live" — real-time climate and sub-national polygons — wrap that script in a small API (e.g. FastAPI) exposing /metrics, /montecarlo, /simulate, and a GeoJSON tile endpoint, and point these screens at it. The math on this page already matches that pipeline cell-for-cell, so it doubles as the reference implementation.
